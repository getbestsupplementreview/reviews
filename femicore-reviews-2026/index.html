    FemiCore Ingredients: Four Botanicals and Five Probiotic Strains

    The label lists nine active ingredients in two blends: 350 mg of herbs and 50 mg of probiotics. One capsule is one serving; each bottle contains 30.

    • Cranberry extract: Standardized to 30% proanthocyanidins (PACs). Some cranberry products have been studied for recurrent UTI prevention, not treatment of active infections.
    • Bearberry leaf (uva ursi): A traditional urinary-use herb containing arbutin-related compounds. Use and duration require care.
    • Berberine hydrochloride: Studied mainly for metabolic health. Evidence for this bladder formula is not established; berberine can interact with medicines.
    • Mimosa pudica seed extract: A traditional botanical with limited human evidence for bladder support.

    The probiotic strains are Lactobacillus crispatus LCr86, L. acidophilus LA88, L. plantarum N13, L. gasseri LG08 and L. casei LC16. Some Lactobacillus strains have urogenital research, but findings may not apply to this blend.

    My main label observation: The ingredients are named, but their individual amounts are not. The probiotic blend is listed by weight, not by colony-forming units (CFU).

    Blend weight is not a CFU count, so probiotic strength cannot be compared with studies.
