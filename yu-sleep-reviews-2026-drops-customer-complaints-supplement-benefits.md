## YU Sleep Side Effects: Is This Supplement Safe?

Natural doesn't automatically mean risk-free.

Melatonin may cause morning drowsiness, headaches, dizziness, or nausea. Its effects can vary between individuals.

The 5-HTP ingredient deserves particular care. Combining it with certain antidepressants or other serotonin-affecting medicines may increase the risk of serotonin syndrome.

I'd recommend professional advice before use for anyone taking prescription medication, pregnant or breastfeeding, or managing an ongoing health condition.

There's another point worth mentioning. Persistent sleeplessness can have several underlying causes. A supplement shouldn't replace a proper medical assessment.
